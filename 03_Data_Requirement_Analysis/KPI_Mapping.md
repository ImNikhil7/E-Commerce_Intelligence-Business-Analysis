# KPI Mapping

## 1. Purpose

This document maps business KPIs to the data required to calculate them.

Only KPIs supported by the available source data will be presented as
observed business metrics.

---

# 2. Sales KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Required Data | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Sum of order-item prices | price | order_items | Supported |
| Monthly Revenue | Revenue grouped by month | price, order date | order_items + orders | Supported |
| Revenue Growth % | Period-over-period revenue change | revenue, date | Derived | Supported |
| Total Orders | Count of orders | order_id | orders | Supported |
| Average Order Value | Revenue / number of orders | revenue, order_id | orders + order_items | Supported |

# 9. Marketing Funnel KPIs

---

# 10. KPIs Currently Not Supported

| KPI | Reason |
|---|---|
| Actual Profit | Product cost is unavailable |
| Profit Margin | Product cost is unavailable |
| Inventory Turnover | Historical inventory data unavailable |
| Stock-out Rate | Historical inventory data unavailable |
| Discount Effectiveness | Reliable discount data unavailable |
| Customer Campaign ROI | Customer campaign cost/attribution unavailable |

These KPIs will not be fabricated.

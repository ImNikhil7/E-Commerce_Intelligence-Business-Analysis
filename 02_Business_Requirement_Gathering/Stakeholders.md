
## Different users need different insights.

| Stakeholder       | Responsibility        | Needs                                   |
| ----------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| CEO               | Company Strategy      | Revenue, Growth, KPIs                   |
| Sales Manager     | Sales Performance     | Product Sales, Monthly Growth           |
| Marketing Manager | Campaigns             | Discount Analysis, Customer Acquisition |
| Inventory Manager | Stock Planning        | Demand Forecasting, Low Stock Alerts    |
| Finance Manager   | Profitability         | Revenue, Profit, Margins                |
| Customer Support  | Customer Satisfaction | Reviews, Complaints, Returns            |

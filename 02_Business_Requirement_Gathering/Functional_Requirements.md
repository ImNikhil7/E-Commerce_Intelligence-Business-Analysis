## Functional requirements of the Project.

### Sales Analysis

The system shall:

- Calculate Total Revenue
- Calculate Monthly Revenue
- Calculate Yearly Revenue
- Calculate Average Order Value
- Identify Best Selling Products
- Identify Lowest Selling Products
- Compare Category Performance

### Customer Analysis

The system shall:

- Identify repeat customers
- Calculate customer purchase frequency
- Identify top spending customers
- Segment customers by spending behavior


### Product Analysis

The system shall:

- Rank products by sales
- Rank products by ratings
- Compare category performance
- Detect slow-moving products

### AI Assistant

The system shall:

- Answer business questions
- Recommend products to promote
- Summarize dashboard insights
- Explain KPI changes

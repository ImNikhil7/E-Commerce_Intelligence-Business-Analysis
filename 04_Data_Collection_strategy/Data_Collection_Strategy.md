## Final Strategy

The project will use a hybrid data collection strategy.

The Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset will serve as
the primary source for observed transactional, customer,
product, payment, review, geographic and delivery data.

Additional external sources will only be introduced when they
provide information that cannot be obtained from the primary
source and can be reliably connected to the analytical model.

Synthetic data may be introduced for specific analytical
scenarios such as inventory simulation, but all synthetic
data will be clearly documented and will not be presented as
observed business data.

The final dataset architecture will be determined after
profiling the selected source and validating its schema.

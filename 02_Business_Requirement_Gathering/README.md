# Phase 2 — Business Requirements

## Purpose

This phase translates the business problem into documented
requirements for analysis, reporting, dashboards, and future
analytical capabilities.

## Documents

- `Business_Requirement_Document.md`
- `Business_Rules.md`
- `Functional_Requirements.md`
- `Non_Functional_Requirements.md`
- `Stakeholders.md`

## Coverage

The requirements address:

- Sales analysis
- Customer analysis
- Product analysis
- Operational analysis
- Marketing analysis
- Customer satisfaction
- Dashboard usability
- Reliability and maintainability
- Future ML/AI capabilities

## Data Feasibility

Not every requested business metric is supported by the selected
dataset.

Where required data is unavailable, the project explicitly documents
the limitation rather than fabricating the metric.

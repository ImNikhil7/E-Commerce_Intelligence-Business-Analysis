## These rules control calculations of businesses.

1. Revenue = Quantity × Selling Price

2. Profit = Revenue − Cost

3. One Order ID belongs to one customer.

4. Every Product belongs to one Category.

5. Products with zero sales for 90 days are considered slow-moving.

6. Cancelled orders are excluded from revenue calculations.

7. Returned orders reduce total revenue.

8. Discount percentage cannot exceed 80%.

9. Average Order Value = Revenue ÷ Number of Orders

10. Orders without valid customer IDs are considered invalid.

# E-Commerce Intelligence Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world data analytics engagement for an e-commerce company called **ShopSphere**.

The objective is to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product and category trends, operational performance, marketing activity, and business KPIs to identify important business patterns and opportunities.

The project also extends traditional business intelligence with a **sales forecasting model** so that historical business performance can be connected with basic predictive analytics.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics lifecycle:

- Business Understanding
- Business Requirement Gathering
- Data Requirement Analysis
- Data Collection Strategy
- Data Profiling
- Data Cleaning
- Business Analysis
- Dashboard Development
- Predictive Analytics
- Business Insights and Recommendations

---

# Business Problem

ShopSphere has a large amount of e-commerce data covering orders, customers, products, payments, reviews, delivery performance, and marketing leads.

The business needs answers to questions such as:

- How is overall sales performance?
- How many orders are being delivered successfully?
- Which product categories generate the most sales?
- How much revenue is affected by freight costs?
- Are customers returning after their first purchase?
- Which customers or locations contribute more value?
- How does delivery performance affect customer satisfaction?
- Which marketing sources generate more closed deals?
- Can historical sales data be used to estimate future sales?

The project converts these business questions into measurable data analysis and interactive Power BI dashboards.

---

# Project Objectives

The main objectives are:

1. Understand the business problem and define analytical requirements.
2. Identify the required data and build a structured analytical dataset.
3. Profile and clean the available data.
4. Perform business analysis across sales, customers, products, delivery, reviews, marketing, and geography.
5. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business reporting.
6. Validate important KPIs and analytical outputs.
7. Build a basic sales forecasting model using historical monthly delivered sales.
8. Evaluate multiple forecasting approaches using a time-based validation process.
9. Make predictive results available inside Power BI.
10. Document the complete analytical workflow so the project can be reproduced and understood by others.

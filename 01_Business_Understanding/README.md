# Phase 1 — Business Understanding

## Purpose

This phase defines the business context, major business problems,
project objectives, scope, stakeholders, and questions that the
ShopSphere analytics project is intended to answer.

## Business Context

ShopSphere is a medium-sized e-commerce marketplace that requires
better visibility into sales, customer behavior, product performance,
operations, and strategic decision-making.

## Key Objectives

- Increase revenue
- Improve customer retention
- Improve operational efficiency
- Support data-driven decision-making
- Identify opportunities for business improvement

## Key Business Questions

The project investigates questions related to:

- Sales and order trends
- Product and category performance
- Customer retention and value
- Geographic concentration
- Delivery performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Marketing-funnel conversion

## Project Scope

The project progresses from business understanding through data
requirements, data collection, profiling, cleaning, validation,
business analysis, KPI development, and decision support.

## Phase Status

**Phase 1 — Complete**

The business context and analytical objectives have been defined
before beginning the data preparation and analysis phases.

# Data Quality Risks

## 1. Missing Values

Some fields may contain missing values.

## 2. Duplicate Records

Duplicate records may exist and must be investigated.

## 3. Referential Integrity

Foreign keys must be validated across the relational datasets.

## 4. Date Consistency

Order, approval, shipping and delivery timestamps must be
checked for logical consistency.

## 5. Anonymization

The source contains anonymized commercial data.

## 6. Historical Limitations

The dataset covers a historical period rather than current
e-commerce activity.

## 7. Missing Business Attributes

Product cost and historical inventory data are not available
in the core source.

## 8. Synthetic Data Risk

Any generated data must be clearly separated from observed
data.
